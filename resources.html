Regenerative development & sustainability

A collection of resources about the environment, with a particular focus on the built environment, for students and researchers interested in regenerative solutions to city building, transportation, and other built-environment problems.

Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change The IPCC’s assessment reports on the science of climate change. A Green New Deal (2008) New Economics Foundation’s original Green New Deal report (archived). Regenesis Group Pioneers of regenerative development and design practice. International Living Future Institute Home of the Living Building Challenge. U.S. Green Building Council The LEED rating system and green-building education. Capital Institute Regenerative economics and the principles of a regenerative economy. Doughnut Economics Action Lab Tools for cities applying Kate Raworth’s doughnut framework. CityCraft Foundation Regenerative city-building in West Denver and beyond. Sun Valley EcoDistrict Master plan for a 100-acre Denver neighborhood guided by the EcoDistricts Protocol (Shears Adkins Rockmore). Sun Valley Redevelopment (DHA) Denver Housing Authority’s replacement of 333 public housing units with 940 new mixed-income homes and an 11-acre riverfront park.

ESG & sustainable investment

GRESB The ESG benchmark for real estate and infrastructure portfolios. CRREM Carbon Risk Real Estate Monitor: decarbonization pathways and stranding-risk tools for buildings. Principles for Responsible Investment The UN-supported investor network for integrating ESG into investment decisions. ISSB (IFRS Sustainability Standards) Global baseline standards for sustainability and climate-related financial disclosure. Global Reporting Initiative Widely used standards for corporate sustainability reporting.

Circular economy & embodied carbon

Ellen MacArthur Foundation Circular-economy principles, case studies, and design for cities. Circularity Gap Report Circle Economy’s annual measure of how circular the global economy is. Building Transparency (EC3) Free calculator for the embodied carbon of construction materials. Architecture 2030 Targets and tools for cutting operational and embodied carbon in the built environment.

Resiliency frameworks

Resilient Cities Network The successor network to the Rockefeller Foundation’s 100 Resilient Cities. UN SDGs — 2019 U.S. Cities Report How the 100 largest U.S. metros perform on the Sustainable Development Goals. Colorado Resiliency Office State resources for community resilience planning.

Real estate, housing & the economy

FRED Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis — thousands of economic series, including rates, home prices, and metro data. data.census.gov American Community Survey and decennial census tables down to the block group. HUD User datasets Fair Market Rents, income limits, CHAS affordability data, and LIHTC properties. FHFA House Price Index Repeat-sales home price indexes for the nation, states, metros, counties, and ZIP codes. Zillow Research data Home value (ZHVI) and rent (ZORI) indexes, inventory, and sales data. Census building permits Monthly and annual new residential construction permits by metro and place. Bureau of Labor Statistics Employment, wages, CPI, and construction-cost data. World Development Indicators The World Bank’s cross-country data on economies, urbanization, and the environment. BEA regional data GDP and personal income by state, county, and metro area. NCREIF The NCREIF Property Index and other institutional real estate performance benchmarks. Nareit data & research REIT index returns, market capitalization, and sector data. NLIHC — Out of Reach The housing wage needed to afford a modest rental in every state, metro, and county. Eviction Lab Eviction filing data and tracking for U.S. cities and states (Princeton).

Spatial, urban & transportation

IPUMS Harmonized census and survey microdata (USA, CPS, international). NHGIS Historical census tables and GIS boundary files from 1790 to the present. Census TIGER/Line Shapefiles for tracts, block groups, roads, and places. LEHD / LODES Where people live and work: block-level origin–destination employment data. EPA Smart Location Database Density, land-use diversity, street design, and transit access for every block group. National Zoning Atlas Standardized, mappable zoning codes across U.S. jurisdictions. Urban Institute Data Catalog Datasets from Urban’s housing, finance, and neighborhood research. Overture Maps Open global data on addresses, buildings, places, and transportation networks in one consistent schema. Microsoft Building Footprints About 1.4 billion machine-detected building outlines worldwide, many with height estimates. Global Building Atlas Worldwide building heights and simple 3D models (TUM); heights are licensed for non-commercial use. Dynamic World Near-real-time, 10-meter global land cover for tracking development, greening, and land-use change. Google Earth Engine Data Catalog Satellite imagery, elevation, land cover, and climate data with free cloud computing for research.

Discussion-based teaching

I use discussion in my topics class, Triple Bottom Line and the Built Environment, to encourage students to engage deeply with material on social justice and environmental sustainability — including by facilitating their own class discussions. As Brookfield and Preskill describe it, the purposes of discussion are fourfold: (1) to help participants reach a more critically informed understanding of the topic, (2) to enhance their self-awareness and capacity for self-critique, (3) to foster an appreciation for the diversity of opinion that emerges when viewpoints are exchanged openly and honestly, and (4) to act as a catalyst for taking informed action in the world.

Stephen Brookfield’s website Works, resources, and tools for facilitating discussion and adult learning, including the Critical Incident Questionnaire. DU Office of Teaching and Learning Resources for teaching and for using technology to support learning. The Discussion Book: 50 Great Ways to Get People Talking A one-page summary of the book’s 50 methods (Columbia CTL).

Brookfield, S. D., & Preskill, S. (2005). Discussion as a Way of Teaching: Tools and Techniques for Democratic Classrooms. John Wiley & Sons.
