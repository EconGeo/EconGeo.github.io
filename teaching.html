I teach real estate and built-environment courses at the University of Denver, with an emphasis on social justice and environmental sustainability in the built environment. My classes lean on discussion-based learning to help students engage deeply with the material, and I use classroom technology to give students the chance to share their work with the world.

Courses

REAL 1700 / 4000

Triple Bottom Line and the Built Environment

A topics course (formerly Business of the Built Environment) examining the environmental, social, and economic dimensions of real estate and city building.

REAL 3307 / 4407

Real Estate Finance / Income Property Finance

Finance and investment analysis for income-producing real estate.

REAL 4800

NAIOP Rocky Mountain Real Estate Challenge

A capstone, competition-based course in which student teams develop a real proposal for the regional NAIOP Real Estate Challenge.

REAL 4701

Real Estate Market Analysis

Methods for analyzing real estate markets, demand, and submarket dynamics.

Graduate · REAL 4000

Students choose a critical issue related to the built environment, outline the primary challenges, discuss how the issue affects the City of Denver, and propose a plan of action for Denver to address it.

Undergraduate · REAL 1700

Students identify a parcel of land in the Denver metro region and propose a redevelopment — a regenerative feasibility study covering stakeholder engagement, a theoretical LEED rating, and the financing of green enhancements.
